About WealthOS

WealthOS was built to bring every side of wealth management — mutual funds, digital gold, fixed income, advisory and back-office operations — onto one platform. We work with investors, advisors, family offices and institutions who need a single, reliable system of record for their money.

Every login is a real, individually authenticated account with role-specific access, structured risk profiling, and full audit trails — because compliance and trust aren't an afterthought in wealth management, they're the whole point.
